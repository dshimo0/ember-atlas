Comparable fire activity

Ember Atlas helps people inspect how MODIS and VIIRS report historical thermal activity. A source-labeled calendar, geographic map and matching exports make each comparison reproducible.

The Pantanal walkthrough replays a sensor-source change and compares adjusted VIIRS with observed MODIS. Eight study areas provide their own observations, coverage and evaluation evidence.

Open, inspectable evidence

Versioned observations, source manifests, transparent adjustment rules and separate evaluation periods support the published results. MODIS is a comparison reference; agreement is not ground-truth accuracy.
