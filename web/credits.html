Sources & AI disclosure

Ember Atlas is an independent project by Diego Shimohama. No NASA endorsement or partnership is claimed.

AI-assisted implementation

OpenAI Codex assisted with application and analysis code, testing, documentation, copy and presentation layout. AI assistance includes generation and revision of source code and explanatory text. The numerical results are computed by that code from versioned NASA FIRMS observations and checked against the published evaluation artifacts. NASA observations were not generated by AI.

Charts are deterministic visualizations of those artifacts. Product images and the silent screen recording are actual browser captures, not generated scenes. Historical synthetic speech used for timing was not published. Owner-provided external AI commentary is treated as unverified research input, not scientific evidence.

Data and analytical provenance

  • NASA FIRMS: MODIS Standard Processing (Terra/Aqua), NOAA-20 VIIRS Standard Processing, and their separately displayed NRT products.
  • NASA MODIS/VIIRS field definitions; literal reported versions and thermal types are preserved.
  • Versioned region registry and validation, source manifests and reproducible methods.

Maps, images and typography

  • Street tiles: © OpenStreetMap contributors; tile usage policy.
  • Optional World Imagery: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community. Service attribution and metadata. This is contextual imagery with varying acquisition dates, not the selected month's thermal observations.
  • Local geography: Natural Earth, public domain. Countries, rivers and settlements; project-defined study rectangles are not official biome masks.
  • Leaflet 1.9.4, BSD-2-Clause license.
  • Editorial NASA images: NASA/GSFC/Reto Stöckli Blue Marble composite (2007) and dated NASA GIBS MODIS reflectance. Exact sources and credits; regional image inventory. Editorial images are separate from analytical measurements.
  • Manrope typeface: Google Fonts repository, SIL Open Font License.
