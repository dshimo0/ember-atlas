REPRODUCE THE RESULT

Methods, scope & provenance

Source & release notes ↗

What is an occupied cell-day?

Detections are put on a fixed globally anchored 0.05° WGS84 grid. Each occupied cell counts once per product per UTC day. Monthly activity is the sum of these occupied cell-days. Physical cell area varies with latitude. MODIS Terra and Aqua are pooled; VIIRS uses NOAA-20 Standard Processing.

Adjustment, remaining bias & limits

The frozen acceptance rule requires lower holdout monthly MAE and no worse absolute signed bias on identical complete paired months: at least 36 training months and 24 holdout months. There is no confidence interval from just two independent holdout years. The transfer is not validated for other regions or outside 2020–2024. No legacy-to-VIIRS handoff is claimed.

Historical median & retrieval coverage

Each sensor is compared with its own complete prior years for the same calendar month, excluding the selected year. At least three prior years are required. Partial months and subregions have no historical anomaly in this release.

Retrieved files do not establish continuous observation. Clouds, overpasses and usable-observation masks remain unmeasured. “No detections recorded” requires successful retrieval; missing data are never filled with zero. Partial selected months are labeled and their adjustment is withheld.

Snapshot, processing & source records

MODIS C6.1 SP and NOAA-20 VIIRS 375 m SP records are deduplicated by product, platform, acquisition time and coordinates. Source fields and original manifests remain in the repository. This static release works without live FIRMS access or browser credentials.
