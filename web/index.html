Methods & data

Source code

Activity metric and native detections

Each occupied cell counts once per product per UTC day on a globally anchored 0.05° WGS84 grid. Monthly activity is the sum of these occupied cell-days; physical cell area varies with latitude. MODIS pools Terra and Aqua; VIIRS uses NOAA-20 Standard Processing.

Native counts are thermal-anomaly detections, not unique fires or burned area. Differences in sensitivity, overpass timing and clouds remain relevant.

Adjustment and validation

The frozen acceptance rule requires lower holdout monthly MAE and no worse absolute signed bias over identical complete paired months: 36 training months and 24 holdout months. MODIS is a comparison reference, not ground truth. The adjustment is limited to complete months in the full study rectangle during 2020–2024. Two independent holdout years do not support a precise uncertainty interval.

Historical baseline and coverage

Historical medians use each sensor's complete prior years for the same calendar month, excluding the selected year, with a minimum of three years. Regional baselines use the same fixed local mask for each prior year. Partial selected months and baselines with fewer than three complete prior years are unavailable.

Retrieval completeness does not establish swath or cloud-free observation coverage. A retrieved zero is distinct from a missing file; unavailable values remain blank. Partial months include only the selected UTC dates.

Snapshot and exports

Observation exports cover the selected month intersected with the date range, area and sensors. Aggregate exports cover the full selected date range. Source fields and original manifests remain in the repository. Map pan and zoom do not change the study-area filter or export scope.

Map sources

The street basemap uses OpenStreetMap tiles, with rivers, settlements and country boundaries. The local fallback uses Natural Earth country boundaries (1:110m), rivers and settlements (1:50m), stored with source checksums. Leaflet 1.9.4 supplies map navigation and scale. The study rectangle is WGS84 59–54°W, 23–15°S. No Pantanal biome geometry is included.
