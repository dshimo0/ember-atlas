Can these sensors be compared?

Methods, provenance and coverage

A thermal anomaly is a satellite detection, not a unique fire or burned area. Both sensors use a globally anchored 0.05° grid; cells vary in physical area with latitude. Each occupied cell counts once per UTC day per product. MODIS Terra and Aqua are pooled. Native observations remain available in the export.

Retrieval completeness is not observation coverage. Clouds, overpass times and detection limits differ. Observation coverage is unknown without swath masks. “No detections recorded” is distinct from unavailable retrieval. Calibration applies only to the full study area and the evaluated period. Historical medians use complete prior years for the same sensor and calendar month; at least three years are required.
