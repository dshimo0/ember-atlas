What does one cell-day mean?

1 cell × 3 days = 3 cell-daysSeveral detections inside the same 0.05° grid cell on one UTC day still count once.

Adding the occupied cells across every day gives monthly activity on a shared counting basis. A finer instrument can still observe more activity. The adjustment is fitted on 2020–2022, then evaluated against separate MODIS observations in 2023–2024.
