Methodology and provenance

Only verified NASA FIRMS extracts enter the snapshot. A successfully retrieved day with no records means “No detections recorded.” Missing retrievals mean “Data unavailable.” Partial months are excluded from historical comparisons. The median requires at least three complete prior years for the same calendar month and sensor, excluding the target year. Subarea filters are uncalibrated.
